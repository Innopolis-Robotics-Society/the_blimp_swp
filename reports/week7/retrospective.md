# Sprint Retrospective (Week 7)

**Project:** Autonomous Indoor Airship Simulation  
**Sprint:** Sprint 5 (Week 7)

---

## What went well

The final sprint successfully concluded the project and resulted in a smooth customer handover.

Positive aspects of the sprint included:

- all Sprint 5 Product Backlog Items were completed before the Sprint Review;
- software, firmware, hardware, and documentation were successfully integrated into a complete working system;
- continuous collaboration between team members enabled parallel progress on implementation, testing, and documentation;
- the final repository review ensured consistency across documentation and project artifacts;
- the customer accepted the delivered project without requesting additional implementation work.

---

## What did not go well

Although the sprint achieved all planned objectives, several process-related challenges were observed.

- Preparing the final documentation required significant coordination because many project artifacts had to be reviewed and synchronized.
- Managing software development, hardware validation, and documentation activities simultaneously required careful planning throughout the sprint.
- Final verification involved multiple interacting components, making troubleshooting more time-consuming than testing individual modules.

These challenges affected the development process rather than the quality of the delivered product.

---

## What the team changed or attempted to change based on the previous Sprint Retrospective, and what results they observed

Based on the previous sprint retrospective, the team focused on maintaining documentation continuously instead of postponing documentation updates until the end of the project.

The team also introduced more frequent repository reviews to ensure that documentation, implementation, and completed Product Backlog Items remained synchronized.

These improvements reduced the amount of rework required during the final sprint, simplified the customer handover process, and contributed to a successful final project review.

---

## Action points

As this sprint concludes the Software Engineering project, no additional sprint action items are planned.

For future teams continuing the project, the following recommendations are provided:

1. Continue maintaining documentation together with implementation to simplify future maintenance and handovers.
2. Preserve the modular architecture and documented development process when introducing new functionality.
