# Week 7 Reflection – Final Delivery

**Project:** Autonomous Indoor Airship Simulation  
**Sprint:** Sprint 5 (Week 7)

---

## Learning points

The final sprint demonstrated the importance of integrating development, testing, and documentation throughout the project rather than treating them as separate activities. Completing the project confirmed several practices that contributed to a successful delivery.

The team learned that:

- maintaining project documentation continuously simplifies the final handover process;
- validating functionality on both the simulation environment and the physical platform significantly increases confidence in the delivered solution;
- regular communication with the customer helps ensure that development remains aligned with project objectives;
- documenting architectural decisions throughout development improves maintainability and onboarding for future contributors;
- a modular system architecture makes integration and testing considerably easier during the final stages of the project.

---

## Validated assumptions

The following assumptions were confirmed during the final Sprint Review and customer handover.

- The Docker-based SITL environment provides a reliable platform for development and testing.
- MAVLink is an appropriate communication protocol for integrating the backend, flight controller, and QGroundControl.
- The selected software architecture supports independent development of individual project components.
- Comprehensive documentation enables the customer to deploy, understand, and continue developing the project independently.
- Continuous customer involvement throughout development improves the quality of the final deliverable.

---

## Friction and gaps

The project objectives were successfully achieved during the final sprint, and no critical functional gaps remained at project completion.

Throughout development, the team identified several opportunities that could be explored if the project is continued in the future.

- Further enhancement of autonomous navigation capabilities.
- Integration of additional localization and perception technologies.
- Support for additional onboard sensors and telemetry sources.
- Expansion of automated testing and continuous integration for future feature development.

These items are considered future enhancements rather than missing functionality.

---

## Planned response

All Sprint 5 Product Backlog Items were completed and accepted by the customer.

Since the Software Engineering project has been successfully completed, no additional sprint work is planned.

If future development continues, the team recommends:

- preserving the modular project architecture;
- maintaining documentation together with implementation;
- extending autonomous flight capabilities incrementally;
- validating new functionality on both simulated and physical platforms.

These recommendations are intended for future maintainers and are outside the scope of the completed course project.
