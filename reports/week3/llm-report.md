# LLM Usage Report

## Tool used
ChatGPT (OpenAI)

## How we used it
**1. Transcription and translation**
We recorded the customer review meeting in Russian. ChatGPT helped us transcribe the audio and translate it into English. We then reviewed the text, fixed small mistakes, and prepared the final version for the repository.

**2. Writing acceptance criteria**
ChatGPT helped us generate initial versions of acceptance criteria for MVP v1 tasks. We reviewed and adjusted them to match the actual requirements.

## Important
All AI-generated content was manually checked by the team. Final decisions, priorities, customer approvals, and technical choices were made by the team.
